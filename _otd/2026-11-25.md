John Sumner Gibson was born on 25 November 1833 in Chester. He was an English priest and a first-class cricketer, though his sporting career was brief. His single recorded first-class match was the 1855 University Match against Oxford, playing for Cambridge University. In that game, he batted low in the order, scoring 8 and 23, and did not bowl.

Gibson was the eldest son of William Gibson, a priest, and Eliza Maria, daughter of John Bird Sumner, Archbishop of Canterbury. The reformer and churchman William Wilberforce was also a relation. After Eliza Maria died in 1836, William Gibson married her cousin, Louisiana, daughter of Charles Sumner, Bishop of Winchester. Many of the thirteen children from these marriages carried 'Sumner' in their forenames, though John Sumner Gibson did not hyphenate his surname.

Educated at Harrow School and Trinity College, Cambridge, Gibson later served as a Church of England priest in Kent, Warwickshire, and Derbyshire until 1873. He then retired to East Sussex, dying in 1892 at Iridge Place, a listed country house near Hurst Green.
