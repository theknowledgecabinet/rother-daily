On 26 November 1947, Charles Tippet collapsed and was found on the floor of his office at Rye Golf Club in East Sussex. He had just finished showing his friend, the Bishop of Ely, around the place. By 1945, Tippet had been given the job of reviving the club, but it proved too much for him. Edith was called to his side, but two days later Major Tippet died at the age of 56.

Tippet had built a career as a golf club secretary and course designer, moving from the United States to various prestigious clubs in England, including Royal Wimbledon and Walton Heath. His death left Edith in reduced circumstances, as he had left no will and only £666 in cash. She subsequently moved to Kent and later Cooden Beach in Sussex.

This event connects to the wider family narrative, as Tippet was the stepfather of Bruce Shand, the paternal grandfather of Queen Camilla.
