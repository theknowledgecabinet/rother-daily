A creative writing exhibition called Riversong is currently on display in Rye train station. The show features poems, riddles, and invocations inspired by walks along the Tillingham, Brede, and Rother rivers.

The work was created by participants in the SongMaps Rye workshop programme between March and July 2026. Local artists also contributed pieces in response to the living waters. The exhibition has been published as an anthology, which is now available for purchase in the Rye Harbour Nature Reserve shop.

According to the Rye News, the writing reflects a range of emotions, including grief and anger at the abuse of the waterways. It aims to rekindle a sense of protectiveness toward the local rivers and transport feelings about nature to a wider audience.
