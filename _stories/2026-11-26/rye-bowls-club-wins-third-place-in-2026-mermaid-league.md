Rye Bowls Club has been awarded third place for the 2026 Mermaid League season. The award was presented at the recent annual league dinner, which was hosted by Judith Blincow at the Mermaid Inn in Rye.

According to Ian Scott of Rye News, the club has steadily improved its position in the league since becoming independent. He noted that the past season saw improvements both on the games front and in the club's social scene.

At the club's recent Annual General Meeting, plans for future developments were shared with the membership. While the playing season will not resume until May next year, social events will continue through the winter period.
