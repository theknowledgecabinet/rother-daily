Plans to convert the former presbytery next to St Mary Magdalene Church in Bexhill into new homes have been refused by Rother District Council planners.

The proposal, submitted by AVF Planning and Design Ltd, sought to create one three-bedroom maisonette, one one-bedroom flat, five two-bedroom flats, and a detached house on the site.

In its decision, the council stated that the change of use would result in the loss of a community facility. It also cited insufficient evidence that the building could not continue in community use and described the proposed roof alterations as unsympathetic to the listed building.
