Rye Film Club is set to return to the Rye Community Centre on Friday 4 December.

The event is scheduled to begin at 7pm, with doors opening at 6.30pm. Admission costs £5.

Rye News has listed the screening as part of its upcoming events guide for the town, noting that Christmas lights are also due to be switched on in Rye on 28 November.
