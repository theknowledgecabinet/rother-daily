Rye Town Council has agreed to support the placement of a bronze Captain Pugwash statue in Gun Gardens, subject to planning approval. The proposal was put forward by the Pugwash Statue Committee, represented by Alan Dickinson, on 25 November. While the idea received unanimous support, councillors debated the exact location to ensure the memorial has maximum effect.

The council also approved an expression of interest for Community Infrastructure Levy funding for the Station Approach public conveniences. Cllr Hughes expressed disagreement with the initial plan for reduced modular facilities, while the Deputy Mayor raised concerns about costs exceeding £320,000. Cllr Hansen and Rebakah Gilbert confirmed the modular approach has been discarded and a final proposal is expected in summer next year.

In other business, Cllr Breeds introduced a proposal for a multi-faith prayer location in Rye. Widespread support was voiced for a peaceful area to serve the town's diverse community. Cllr Smith Ellis cited airport prayer rooms as an example of providing tranquility in busy locations.
