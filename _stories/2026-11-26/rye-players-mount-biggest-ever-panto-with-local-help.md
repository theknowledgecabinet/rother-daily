Rye Players are preparing to stage their largest town pantomime yet, Cinderella, in December. The production is being built on the goodwill of local residents and professionals who have donated their time and skills.

Ben from Rye Harbour gifted professional decking and staging treads, while Phil Clarke, recently head of production at RADA, is advising the stage manager on set safety. Owen Thompson is choreographing the entire production, and local artist Charlotte Brooks designed the show's poster.

Shirley Brody, a professional hair and make-up artist with West End credits, is styling the cast, while Mark Russell is creating costumes from the company's collection. Bespoke florist Maisie Golding-Cocozza has provided urn arrangements for the ball scene. The article notes that the cast and backstage crew are all local volunteers juggling their everyday lives with rehearsals.

This year, a portion of ticket sales will benefit the Rye Food Bank. The production highlights the community effort required to bring a major theatrical event to the town, with all professional expertise freely given.
