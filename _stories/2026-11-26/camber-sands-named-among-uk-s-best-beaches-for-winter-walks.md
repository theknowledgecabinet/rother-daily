Camber Sands has been named one of the best beaches in the UK for a winter walk. The site, which features the only sand dune system in East Sussex, was included on Beachlets' list of the top 10 beaches for the season.

The website highlighted the beach's wide shoreline at low tide, which offers plenty of room for long walks. It noted that the open setting is ideal for a brisk walk on a clear winter day, though it can be windy. Beachlets also recommended visiting nearby Rye for its cobbled streets and independent shops, or exploring Rye Harbour Nature Reserve for wintering birds.

Camber Sands is rated 4.3 out of 5 on Tripadvisor based on more than 2,000 reviews. Visitors have praised the "wind-swept" atmosphere and the space to get away from crowds, with one reviewer describing a visit in December as a way to "clear your head and fill your heart".
