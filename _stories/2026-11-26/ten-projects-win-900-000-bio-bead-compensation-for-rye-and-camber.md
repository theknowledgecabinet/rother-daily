Ten local projects have been selected to share £900,000 in reparation funding from Southern Water. The money follows the accidental release of plastic bio-beads from the company’s Eastbourne wastewater treatment works in October 2025.

Residents in Rye and Camber voted for the winners at public events held on 17 and 18 October. More than 180 people attended the two sessions to examine proposals and cast their votes. The successful schemes include a beach safety drone, a quad bike for emergency response, and a late-night bus service linking Camber and Lydd with Rye railway station.

Other funded projects cover habitat improvements at Rye Harbour Nature Reserve, a replacement lifeboat for Pett Level Independent Rescue Boat, and the establishment of a Camber Parkrun. George Taylor, director of wastewater operations at Southern Water, said the process was designed to put decisions in the hands of local people.
