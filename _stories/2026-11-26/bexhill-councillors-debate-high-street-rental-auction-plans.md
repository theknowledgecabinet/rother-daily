Bexhill Town Council has discussed Rother District Council’s proposal to use High Street Rental Auction powers in the town centre. The scheme aims to bring persistently vacant commercial properties back into use.

Councillors raised questions about who would pay for repairs and insurance on empty units. Cllr Alan Goss noted that many long-term vacancies are not in good condition, while Cllr Memish Huseyin opposed the plans, suggesting they could raise significant issues.

Cllr Paul Wilson clarified the proposal affects only the central part of Bexhill, covering 12 per cent of empty properties. Cllr Ian Hollidge argued the town council should support giving the district council the powers to trial the scheme in selected locations.

A recent audit found 55 vacant units out of 439 surveyed in the town centre, a 12.5 per cent vacancy rate. Cabinet member for regeneration Christine Bayliss said the council wants an evidence-led approach, learning from other early adopters before making further decisions.
