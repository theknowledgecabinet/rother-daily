Rother District Council’s Overview and Scrutiny Committee met on 16 November to review the council’s financial position and performance for the second quarter of 2026/27. A report to the committee, prepared by the Director of Resources, forecasts a revenue surplus of £668,000 at the end of the year. This surplus is driven by favourable financing costs.

The committee also noted that the council’s capital programme is forecast at £194m, with £41m spent in the current year. The council’s savings targets for 2026/27 are being monitored, with £477,700 anticipated to be fully deliverable and £86,800 identified as undeliverable in the current year.

In a separate performance report, the council’s net direct emissions rose by 40% to 51.61 TCO2e, exceeding the target by 48.54 TCO2e. Income from assets is currently below target mainly due to voids, while the average cost of placing a household in temporary accommodation has increased due to market supply and utility costs.
