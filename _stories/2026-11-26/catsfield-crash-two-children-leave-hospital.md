Two children, aged six and three, have been released from hospital after being struck by a car in Catsfield, Sussex Police said.

The crash occurred at about 08:30 GMT on Wednesday on the B2240 at The Stream. A 42-year-old man was also taken to hospital with serious injuries and remains there, police said.

A 72-year-old man was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving and driving while unfit through drink or drugs. He has been bailed while inquiries continue, Sussex Police said.

The road has reopened after being closed for the investigation. Officers asked anyone with information or footage to come forward, quoting Operation Westcott.
