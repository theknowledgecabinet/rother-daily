Hazel Lodge Care Home in Battle hosted a Tommy Tea Party to raise funds for the Royal British Veterans Enterprise charity.

The event brought together residents, relatives, staff, and local veterans, alongside volunteers from The Battle Poppy Appeal and members of the local charity Veterans Growth.

Guests enjoyed an afternoon tea with live music from singer Laura, who performed wartime classics, followed by a quiz where the Battle Poppy Volunteers took the prize.

Rikki-Gene Bury, General Manager at Hazel Lodge, said the event was a "huge amount of fun" and that the community had been supportive of the cause.
