Children from Battle Abbey Nursery in Bexhill recently visited Southlands care home to celebrate a resident’s 104th birthday. The preschool group, known as the Oaks, created a card featuring 104 balloons made from their fingerprints and performed a rendition of Happy Birthday.

According to the nursery, the resident was seated in a throne with a glass of Ribena, surrounded by gold decorations. The visit was described as a moment that brought generations together in a joyful celebration.
