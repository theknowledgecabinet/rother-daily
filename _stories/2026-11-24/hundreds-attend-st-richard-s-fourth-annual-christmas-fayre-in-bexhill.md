St Richard's Catholic College in Bexhill hosted its annual Christmas Fayre on Saturday, 21 November, with hundreds of people attending the event.

The day, now in its fourth year, was organised by the Parent, Teacher, and Friends Association (PTFA) and featured more than 40 independent stallholders, festive food and drinks, and entertainment.

Helen Dorling, PTFA chair, said the event started as an alternative to an outdoor summer fayre due to unpredictable weather. She noted that the gathering has grown each year, raising money for the school while fostering a sense of community spirit that she feels is increasingly rare.
