An effigy of comedian Russell Brand was set alight during the bonfire celebrations in Robertsbridge on Saturday, November 21. The event saw the village’s Station Road and High Street described as a "river of fire" as the procession moved through the streets.

The torchlit march began at the station at 7.15pm, led by the Robertsbridge Society in costumes of monks and Roman centurions. The group made its way to the Recreation Ground, where a giant bonfire was lit, followed by a fireworks display.

Robertsbridge is known for its impressive bonfire effigies, with last year’s event targeting Prince Andrew after he was stripped of his royal title. The society has already launched a fundraiser for the 2027 event, asking for donations to help make next year’s celebration bigger and better.
