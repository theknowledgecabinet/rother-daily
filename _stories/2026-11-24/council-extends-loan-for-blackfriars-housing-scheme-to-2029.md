Rother District Council has agreed to extend the Development Facility Agreement for its housing company until 31 July 2029. The decision was made by the Shareholder Executive Committee on 18 November at the Town Hall in Bexhill-on-Sea.

The extension provides flexibility for the Blackfriars programme, where final completions are now expected in May 2029. Officers recommended the change to allow scope for any further delays without causing issues with the transfer of homes.

The council’s financial exposure on the project reached a peak of £38.6m in October 2026, against a total loan facility of £40m. While 130 homes were pre-sold to Places for People, the sale of the remaining 70 open market dwellings has been slower than anticipated due to weak market conditions and higher borrowing costs.
