The distinctive staircase windows at Bexhill’s De La Warr Pavilion have been removed as major internal refurbishment work begins. Scaffolding and security fencing are now in place around the area, with the curved glass and metal frames taken down for specialist restoration.

A spokesperson for the pavilion said the work is part of the “An Icon For Everyone” project, a partnership with Rother District Council and Haworth Tompkins architects. They explained that the north and south staircases are in disrepair, with cracks in the glass expanding during storms and signs of corrosion on the original single-glazed windows.

Specialist heritage contractor Associated Steel Window Services is handling the repairs. The windows have been transported to a workshop in Surrey for restoration, which includes installing new steel ring beams, curved metal beading, and new glazing to improve the building’s airtightness and long-term resilience.
