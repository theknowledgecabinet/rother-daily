Award-winning jazz guitarist Nigel Price is set to make his first appearance at the Blue Note Jazz Club in Bexhill on Sunday 6 December.

John Fordham of The Guardian noted, "Wes Montgomery guitar fans need look no further than the Uk's Nigel Price." His jazz career spans more than 30 years and he has performed more than 600 times at London’s Ronnie Scott’s Jazz Club. He has played on over 70 albums, including 12 as a leader.

Price will be joined by Mark Fletcher, the house drummer at Ronnie Scott’s for more than 20 years, and Nigel Thomas on double bass, a staple of the Brighton jazz scene for over 35 years. Lynda Murray, a saxophonist, will host the afternoon session.

The event is scheduled from 3pm to 5pm at the club on London Road, near the town hall. Tickets cost £15, payable by cash or card on the door, with the bar opening at 2.30pm.
