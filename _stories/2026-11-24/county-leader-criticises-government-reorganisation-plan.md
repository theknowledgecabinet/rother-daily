East Sussex County Council has responded to the Government's call for further evidence on local government reorganisation. Cllr Andy Woolley, the council leader, stated that the Government has not explained the reasons for its proposal to create a new unitary authority for East Sussex, excluding East Saltdean, Peacehaven and Telscombe.

The council leader said the plan would transfer 26,000 people to Brighton and Hove without evidence that this benefits residents. He noted that the Government appears to be ignoring feedback from local residents who have raised concerns and rejected the plan. The council argued that breaking apart established public services, including social care, would be complex, costly and could put vulnerable residents at risk.

The council said it will resubmit its evidence during the current representation period. It hopes the Government will reconsider its decision, which it described as piling costs onto local people who are already struggling.
