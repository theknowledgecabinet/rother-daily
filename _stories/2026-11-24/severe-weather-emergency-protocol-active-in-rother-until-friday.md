Rother District Council has activated its Severe Weather Emergency Protocol (SWEP) in response to current and forecasted weather conditions. The protocol is a locally agreed procedure designed to minimise the risk of harm to people sleeping rough by providing emergency accommodation.

The council stated that SWEP will remain active until Friday, 27 November. It is an offer for rough sleepers in Rother who do not have access to accommodation and require assistance during severe weather.

Those needing help should approach the council at the Town Hall on London Road, Bexhill on Sea, during office hours. If unable to attend in person, residents can call 01424 787000 during office hours or 0330 123 3517 for out-of-hours assistance.
