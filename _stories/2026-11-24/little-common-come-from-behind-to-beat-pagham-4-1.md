Little Common secured a 4-1 victory over Pagham at the Recreation Ground on Saturday, securing three much-needed points in the SCFL Premier.

The hosts fell behind in the 10th minute after failing to clear a free kick, but responded with a spectacular strike from Ollie MacDonald in the 23rd minute to level the score. Kieran Ludlow added a header before the break, while Freddie Warren and Sonny Tyrell scored in the second half to complete the turnaround.

Manager Russ Eldridge said the team showed "great character" to respond to the early deficit. He noted that while the early goal was disappointing, the squad's attitude and work rate were key to the win.
