The newly restored Rye Literary Institute is set to hold its first ever Festive Market on Saturday 5 and Sunday 6 December.

Jane Fitch, one of the organisers, describes the event as "quite an experience". The market will feature a curated group of local and regional makers, offering items such as Christmas wreaths, knitwear, ceramics, and wood turning.

Visitors can also enjoy food and drink from Mermaid Street Café and Oxney Organic Estate. Live music will play on both days, and cartoonist David Simonds will be selling original artworks and offering hand-drawn portraits of pets from photos.
