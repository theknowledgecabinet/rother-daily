Shops in St Leonards Road and the lower end of Sea Road in Bexhill stayed open until 7pm last Friday, 20 November, for a late-night shopping event. The initiative aimed to bring Christmas cheer to residents and visitors.

The idea came from SageBay & Co dress shop, supported by the St Leonards Road Community Association. Shoppers were entertained by the Sussex Brass Band and welcomed with treats including prosecco, sausage rolls, and sweets.

Janine du Plessis, chair of the association and owner of Jaadu, said: “We just wanted to tempt people to find us.” She noted that some residents prefer shopping when it is peaceful. The association meets monthly to find ways to improve the road and welcomes all shopkeepers and residents.
