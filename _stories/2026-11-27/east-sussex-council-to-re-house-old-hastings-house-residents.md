East Sussex County Council is working to find new accommodation for residents of Old Hastings House care home in Hastings after failing to agree on increased placement costs.

The council said it was "unable to support" a requested fee increase of between £550 and £600 per person per week, which would have added approximately £800,000 to its annual expenditure.

Old Hastings House, which offers residential care and secure dementia support, said it had been engaged in discussions with the council for several months. The home stated that the proposed increases were necessary to bridge the gap between its costs and the rates currently paid by the local authority.

Helena Dollimore, MP for Hastings and Rye, told the House of Commons that 28 residents had been given "the worst news in the run-up to Christmas" and called for more time to find new homes.
