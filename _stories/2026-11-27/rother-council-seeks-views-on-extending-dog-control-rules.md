Rother District Council has launched a six-week consultation on its Public Space Protection Order (PSPO) for dogs. The current order is due to expire in January 2027, and the council proposes to extend it for a further three years until 2030.

Residents, dog owners, walkers, and local businesses are invited to share their views. The council is specifically asking whether a new requirement should be introduced for people to carry suitable means of cleaning up after their pets in public spaces.

Councillor Nicola McLaren, the council’s portfolio holder for environmental services, said the aim is to keep public spaces welcoming, safe, and clean. She encouraged anyone who uses parks, beaches, or open spaces to take part in the process to help shape future rules.
