Rother District Council has approved plans to convert the public toilets building at Little Common Roundabout in Bexhill into a cafe. The decision was made by planning officers who reviewed the application from Jessica Jones of Crema Del Cafe Ltd.

According to the council, an accessible toilet will be retained on the site. It will remain open to non-customers and be available outside the cafe’s opening hours for RADAR key holders. The council stated that retaining the facility would continue to support community needs.

The building, located behind a bus shelter, will be fitted with new doors and windows. The interior will be remodelled to provide indoor seating and counter space, while an outdoor seating area and gated bin storage will also be added. The council noted that the development would support the local economy and create a small number of jobs.
