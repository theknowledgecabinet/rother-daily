John Hobbs, an Army veteran in his 81st year, is set to walk 40 miles from Rye to raise funds for two local charities. He will start at 6am on Saturday 28 November, walking towards Lydd and back, then to Camber and back twice. The route includes checkpoints at Jury's Gap and the entrance to the Lydd military training area.

Mr Hobbs served with the Royal Army Medical Corps in the 1960s and has undertaken charity walks since 1984. This walk supports the East Sussex Veterans Hub and the Hastings and Rother branch of the Association of Ukrainians in Great Britain. Representatives of both charities will accompany him along the route.

Khrystyna Mashchenska, chair of the AUGB branch, said local businesses agreed to act as donation points without hesitation. "Nobody seemed to hesitate for a second," she said. Donations can be made online until 5 December or in cash at shops in Bexhill and Hastings.
