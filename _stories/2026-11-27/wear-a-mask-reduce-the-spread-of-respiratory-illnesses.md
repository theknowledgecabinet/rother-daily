East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust is asking people to wear face masks in some areas of its hospitals and services.

The trust, which runs Conquest Hospital in Rye and Bexhill Hospital, says it is seeing a sharp rise in respiratory illnesses, including flu and COVID. Masks are now requested in emergency departments and some areas where patients are admitted directly.

Visitors may also be asked to wear a mask on wards where respiratory viruses are present. The trust says masks will be available in these areas and staff will let people know when they are needed.
