Hundreds of Fieldfares and Redwings are arriving around Rye from Scandinavia and Siberia to escape harsh northern weather. David Bentley, writing for Rye News, notes that these winter thrushes are currently settling into the area as autumn colours peak.

Bentley says the best places to spot them are the orchards at Iden and Peasmarsh, as well as berry-laden bushes near pasture. He also points to the trees along Station Road in Winchelsea and the hedgerows leading out to Walland Marsh as favoured haunts.

While Fieldfares are described as intensely beautiful with orange chests, Redwings are smaller with a distinctive cream eye stripe. Bentley observes that if a cold snap arrives and fruit supplies dwindle, the birds may lose their shyness and move closer to humans, with Redwings potentially appearing in local gardens.
