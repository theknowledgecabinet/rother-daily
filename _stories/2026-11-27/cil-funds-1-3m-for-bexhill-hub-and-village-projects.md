Rother District Council has allocated £1.3 million from the Community Infrastructure Levy to local projects. The CIL is a charge on developments over a certain size to fund the building and upgrading of public facilities that will be needed to support growth.

The largest award goes to Rother Voluntary Action for a new multi-use community hub in Bexhill. This space will host activities, events, and training, as well as local services. East Sussex County Council will receive £310,000 to create a Special Educational Needs and Disabilities facility at Glenleigh Park Primary Academy in Bexhill.

Catsfield Parish Council will use £35,748 to replace the pavilion's oil-fired boiler, install solar panels, and improve insulation. Westfield Parish Council was awarded £35,000 for traffic calming and public space improvements along the A28. Other beneficiaries include Flatlands Projects Art Centre, Bexhill Museum, and Rye Leisure Centre.
