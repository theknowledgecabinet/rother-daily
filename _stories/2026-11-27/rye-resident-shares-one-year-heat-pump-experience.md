Andrew Pickles, a Rye resident, has published his first-year experience of living with a heat pump system. He notes that while installation costs are high and the process can be disruptive, the system has been almost entirely positive for his 1960s house.

Pickles explains that heat pumps work best with large hot-water tanks and larger radiators, rather than small tanks of very hot water. He advises homeowners to ask installers for references and to consider doing other renovations simultaneously to manage the disruption.

The article highlights that while the unit is not silent, the noise is manageable in Rye. Pickles also notes that air-to-water heat pumps cannot cool the house in summer, a limitation he did not anticipate. He offers to show visitors how the system works by email.
