A date has been announced for when a new community hub in Bexhill town centre is expected to open. Work has started on the building, located at 34 Devonshire Road, where scaffolding has appeared outside the premises. More than £519,000 has been allocated to support the creation of the hub at the former Edinburgh Woollen Mill building.

Rother District Council (RDC) said the money was awarded to Rother Voluntary Action (RVA) from Rother’s Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL) fund. Cllr Christine Bayliss, RDC's cabinet member for economic development and regeneration, noted that this funding is on top of the funding awarded from the Government to purchase the building in 2023. She said, “Fingers crossed the revamped ground floor will be ready to open in Spring 2027.”

A spokesperson for RVA said the project aims to transform the building into a welcoming, accessible community space for Bexhill and Rother, creating a long-term community asset while preserving an important part of the high street.
