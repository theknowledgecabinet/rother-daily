Bexhill-on-Sea’s Christmas Light Switch-On and Christmas Market is scheduled for Saturday, 5 December. The event will take place on Western Road and Devonshire Square, running from 3pm until 7pm.

According to Bexhill-on-Sea Town Council, the celebration is held in association with the Bexhill Lions Club. The day begins at 3pm with an opening address from The Town Crier, who will also announce the winner of the Christmas Window Competition.

The main ceremony, featuring the Mayor of Bexhill-on-Sea and the Christmas Town Cry, is set for 5:30pm. The council noted that a full line-up of musical and theatrical acts will be announced soon.
