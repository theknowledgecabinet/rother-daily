A 72-year-old man has been arrested following a serious collision between a car and three pedestrians in Catsfield. The incident occurred on The Stream at approximately 8.30am on Wednesday, 25 November.

Sussex Police said the man was arrested on suspicion of causing serious injury by dangerous driving and driving while unfit through drink or drugs. He remains in custody. Two children and a man were taken to hospital for treatment.

The road remains closed at the junctions with Church Road and near Watermill Stream. Police said enquiries remain ongoing.
