Beech Estate Campsite in Battle has been named the most unique site in the UK at the 2026 Campsites.co.uk Camping and Glamping Awards.

The site is set within 600 acres of woodland on a private 2,000-acre estate, offering an off-grid experience with bell tents and hammocks. It has earned a 4.81-star rating, with 96 per cent of guests saying they would recommend it.

Beech Estate said its combination of secluded woodland pitches, traditional campfires, and warm bucket showers made it stand out. Hale Farm Campsite in Chiddingly also took runner-up spots for Best Campsite and Best Family Campsite in South East England.
