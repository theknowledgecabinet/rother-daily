A new dedicated cancer support hub is arriving at Eastbourne District General Hospital. The facility will provide information, practical guidance, and emotional support for patients and families across East Sussex.

East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust said the hub was made possible by a £200,000 grant from Macmillan Cancer Support, which funded the entire project. The bespoke building was constructed off-site in Lancashire and is due to be delivered to the hospital on Saturday 28 November.

The hub will offer signposting, social prescribing, and a private counselling room for cancer teams. It is designed to give patients and families a space to talk away from busy clinical areas, supporting them from diagnosis through to end-of-life care.
