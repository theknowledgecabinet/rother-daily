Dr Kieran Mullan, the Conservative MP for Bexhill and Battle, spoke in the House of Commons on Tuesday 24 November regarding the Civil Service Pension Scheme.

Speaking in the chamber, he stated that several of his constituents, including Karen Allum and Janette Woolgar, face several months of delays in their payments. He asked the Minister what deadline has been set for Capita to resolve the issue and meet reasonable service standards.

The MP also voted on the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill, voting against a motion to disagree with a Lords amendment, which passed 260 to 98. He voted in favour of an amendment to the same bill, which was defeated 93 to 278. He voted No on a related Armed Forces Bill motion.
