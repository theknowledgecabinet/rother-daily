The Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman has upheld a complaint against East Sussex County Council regarding the handling of a child’s Education, Health and Care (EHC) Plan. The decision, issued on 13 October 2026, found fault in how the council managed the case between February 2025 and March 2026.

The Ombudsman found that the council fettered its discretion by rigidly applying a policy that prevents amending an EHC Plan within 12 months of a Tribunal decision. It failed to properly consider information from a plan review and did not adequately assess its duty to provide alternative educational provision in early 2026. This approach caused distress and uncertainty for the family.

The council has agreed to apologise to the parent and make a symbolic payment. It will also review its policy to ensure it fully considers information gathered during plan reviews. The Ombudsman will share this decision with Ofsted.
