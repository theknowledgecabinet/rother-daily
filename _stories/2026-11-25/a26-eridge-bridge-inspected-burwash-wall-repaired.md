East Sussex Highways has completed principal inspections of the A26 bridge over the railway at Eridge. The work required special closures of the railway line, arranged through Network Rail, to allow inspectors to access the structure safely.

A lorry fitted with a special platform was used to examine the bridge while traffic lights controlled passing vehicles. This method allowed the team to get a close-up view of the structural elements to prepare a detailed condition report.

In Burwash, the team also repaired cracks in the parapet wall above the river on Willingford Lane. They used crack stitching, which involves cutting slots between bricks and fixing reinforcing bars with specialist grout to strengthen the wall without removing good brickwork.
