Crowhurst Park Lodges in Battle has been named the Relax & Explore Small Park destination in the South of England at Hoseasons’ 21st annual Diamond Awards.

The awards recognise UK holiday parks, lodge resorts and boating locations rated highest by guests during the 2026 season. The ceremony took place at Celtic Manor Resort in Newport, South Wales.

A spokesperson for the park said the team always goes above and beyond to ensure guests have a fantastic break. Lloyd Sceats-Page, account director at Hoseasons, described the team as richly deserving winners who consistently go the extra mile to delight guests.
