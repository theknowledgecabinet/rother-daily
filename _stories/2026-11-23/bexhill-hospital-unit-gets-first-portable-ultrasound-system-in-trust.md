The Bexhill Irvine Unit has received a new SonoSite MT Ultrasound System, the first of its kind introduced within East Sussex Healthcare NHS Trust. The portable device is designed to support specialist rehabilitation and injection services for patients with complex needs following a stroke, brain injury, or neurological diagnosis.

Rehabilitation Consultant Karen Poole said the technology allows for real-time imaging at the point of care. This ensures treatments, such as Botulinum Toxin injections for spasticity, are delivered with increased precision and safety. The compact design also supports complex cannulation and image-guided joint injections.

The trust said the investment helps reduce the need for patients to transfer to acute sites for these procedures. By keeping care local, the unit aims to improve treatment effectiveness and support best practice standards in musculoskeletal and rehabilitation care across East Sussex.
