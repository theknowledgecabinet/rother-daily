A licensing panel has approved a Premises Licence for Battle Abbey School Boarding House on Whatlington Road. The licence covers alcohol sales, late night refreshment, and regulated entertainment, primarily for weddings and functions during school holidays. Hours are set from 12:00 to 00:30, Monday to Sunday. A report to the committee notes that eight representations objected to the application, citing concerns about crime, public nuisance, safety, and child protection. The officer recommendation was to determine the application, and the panel approved it.

Three meetings were held this week. At the Licensing and General Purposes Committee on 16 November, Tony Biggs (Conservative) and Polly Gray (Green Party) attended. Jimmy Stanger (Conservative) sent apologies. At the Overview and Scrutiny Committee on 16 November, nine councillors attended: four Conservatives, two Liberal Democrats, one Labour, one Green, and one Rother Association of Independent Councillors. Apologies were received from Charles Clark (Independent), Sam Coleman (Labour), and Kathryn Field (Liberal Democrats). The Shareholder Executive Committee met on 18 November with eight attendees: three Rother Association of Independent Councillors, two Labour, two Liberal Democrats, and one Green. Apologies were sent by Teresa Killeen (Liberal Democrats) and Ruairi McCourt (Labour).

The council has 38 councillors. The Conservative group has 13 members, led by Carl Maynard. The Liberal Democrats have 8, led by Susan Prochak. Labour has 6, led by Gareth Delany. The Rother Association of Independent Councillors has 6, led by Doug Oliver. The Green Party has 3, led by Polly Gray. Two independents serve on the council. Cabinet members include Doug Oliver (Leader), Susan Prochak (Deputy Leader), and others from various parties.

The Overview and Scrutiny Committee reviewed the second quarter performance report. A report to the committee says net direct emissions rose 40% to 51.61 TCO2e, exceeding the target. Housing performance was described as excellent, with an 80% increase in households prevented from homelessness. The committee also reviewed the budget, which forecasts a revenue surplus of £668,000. Capital works at the De La Warr Pavilion are delayed to March 2027, and Skinners Shed faces delays due to asbestos. The Shareholder Executive Committee reviewed the financial position of the Rother DC Housing Company. A report to the committee says peak debt reached £38.6m in October 2026. It recommends extending the Development Facility Agreement until July 2029 to allow for delays in final completions.

Upcoming meetings include the Climate Change Steering Group on 26 November, which will discuss district-wide emissions and the Climate Action Plan. The Audit Committee meets on 30 November to review procurement and internal audit reports. The Licensing Panel meets on 1 December to consider a Premises Licence application for Mermaidian Rye Tea Rooms in Rye. The Standards Committee also meets on 1 December, though the agenda is not yet published.
