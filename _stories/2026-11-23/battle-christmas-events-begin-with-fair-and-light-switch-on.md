Battle Town Council has published a schedule of festive events for the town, starting on Saturday 28 November. The day features a Taste of Sussex at Battle Abbey School, a Christmas Fair at St Mary’s Church, and Santa’s High Street Parade.

The festivities continue with Santa’s Grotton in the Memorial Hall, fairground rides on Abbey Green, and the Christmas Light Switch On at 5pm. On Sunday 29 November, St Mary’s Church hosts a Winter Wonderland Christmas Tree Festival.

Further events include late-night shopping on 3 and 11 December, a Christmas Market in Market Square, and a Tractor Run on 20 December. The council noted that free parking will be available on 28 November, 5 December, 12 December, and from 4pm on 11 December.
