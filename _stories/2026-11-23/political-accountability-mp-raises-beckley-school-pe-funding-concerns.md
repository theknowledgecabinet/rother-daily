Dr Kieran Mullan, the Conservative MP for Bexhill and Battle, raised a local issue in the Commons on Monday 16 November. He spoke during Topical Questions about the impact of government reforms on physical education funding at Beckley Church of England primary school. He stated that teachers and parents reported a "massive drop" in provision and asked the Minister to agree to meet him to discuss the situation in Beckley and other schools.

The MP participated in 8 of the 10 recorded Commons votes this week. He did not vote on the Immigration and Asylum Bill Third Reading or the Government Amendment on the Rosebank oil field. He voted against the Visitor Levy (England) Bill, which passed 296 to 96. He also voted against several new clauses in the Immigration and Asylum Bill Report Stage, all of which were defeated. He voted in favour of the Opposition motion on the Rosebank oilfield and the UK-EU relations debate, both of which were defeated. He voted against the Draft Employment Rights Act regulations, which passed 299 to 159. He voted against his party's position 0 times.

Dr Mullan serves as the Shadow Minister for Justice. He is a member of the Conservative Party.

The MP made six spoken contributions. On Tuesday 17 November, he asked the Minister to meet him and the MP for Bishop Auckland to discuss using a North Sea wealth fund for deep geothermal projects. On Wednesday 18 November, he spoke in debates on the Rosebank oilfield and UK-EU relations. He asked what percentage contribution Rosebank would make to global warming and questioned the security risks of relying on China for critical minerals. He also received three written answers. The Ministry of Defence stated it had not held discussions with Wealden District Council about cadet facilities at Crowborough Training Camp. The Home Office said it engages regularly with the Council regarding the camp's use. The Department for Energy Security and Net Zero confirmed it is accelerating low carbon technologies, including geothermal.
