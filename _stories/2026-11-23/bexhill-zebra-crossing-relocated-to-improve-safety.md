A zebra crossing in Bexhill town centre has been relocated further south, the county council said. The move allows motorists to complete a full turn from Endwell Road before stopping at the crossing, improving safety for pedestrians.

East Sussex County Council lead member for transport and environment Cllr Peter Griffiths said the area is now safer for all road users. He noted the crossing is used by pupils walking to and from St Richard’s Catholic College.

Additional work on Sea Road included carriageway patching, footway reconstruction, and drainage improvements. New road markings and signage were installed to ensure the area is clearly laid out.
