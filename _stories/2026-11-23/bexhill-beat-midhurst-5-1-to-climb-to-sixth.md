Bexhill put five past Midhurst and Easebourne in the latest in a long run of Polegrove fixtures which has seen the Pirates move steadily up the table. The win continues a strong run of form for the Pirates, who have been climbing steadily since September.

According to the Hastings Observer, Bexhill started brightly with Charlie Playford scoring in the first minute and Ash Latuske setting up Shonk for the second. Just before the break, Latuske lashed in a drive that had the keeper beaten all ends up but the ball somehow bounced back out into play off the inside of the post.

Midhurst pulled one back early in the second half, but Bexhill responded with goals from Theo Coombs and Shonk. Substitute Jack Samways added a fifth goal with a half-volley in the 76th minute, sealing the result.
