A two-vehicle collision involving a motorcycle and a car occurred just before 7.40am on Monday, 23 November, on the A259 between Eastbourne and Bexhill.

A spokesperson for Sussex Police said one of those involved sustained a minor injury. The accident caused long delays and congestion in both directions at the B2095 Hooe turn-off.

According to AA Traffic News, the congestion extended to the Pevensey Roundabout and over two miles back into Bexhill to Little Common.
