Tributes have been paid to Kenneth Stevens, a well-known figure in Bexhill who died peacefully in his sleep on November 6, aged 90, at the Conquest Hospital. His niece, Kate, said he was a beloved local presence, often remembered for writing, performing in, and making costumes for pantomimes.

Mr Stevens grew up at 136 London Road, Bexhill, and attended the Bexhill Downs School for Boys. He was the brother of Eileen Stevens, who served as mayor of Bexhill three times and was the longest-serving magistrate in Sussex at the time of her death. Kate noted that his passing occurred exactly 25 years to the day after his sister’s death.

Beyond his theatrical work, Mr Stevens had a varied career, including roles as an air steward for British European Airways and a stallholder at Rye Market for 27 years selling antiques. He also operated shops in Bexhill and St Leonards, including an antique shop at 92 Bohemia Road and a fancy dress shop in King's Road.
