East Sussex County Council confirmed decisions on transport and adult social care this week. The Lead Member for Transport and Environment noted decisions made on 20 July 2026 regarding the Pan-Sussex Minerals and Waste Plan Proposal and the Highway Vegetation Management Policy review. The Lead Member for Adult Social Care and Health confirmed decisions on residential and nursing provider payments, the Integrated Health and Wellbeing Service contract, and Disability Related Expenditure.

Six meetings were held this week. The Lead Member for Transport and Environment meeting on 16 November had one Reform UK councillor present. The SBSA Audit and Governance Committee on 17 November had eight councillors present and one apology. The Lead Member for Adult Social Care and Health meeting on 19 November had one Reform UK councillor present. The County Consultative Committee, Discretionary Transport Appeal Panel, and East Sussex Schools Forum also met, with no attendance figures recorded in the minutes for the latter three.

The council has 50 councillors. Reform UK holds 20 seats, the Liberal Democrats 14, the Green Party 10, the Conservatives 4, and Independents 2. Andy Woolley (Reform UK) is Leader of the Council. Kathryn Field (Liberal Democrats) leads the Liberal Democrat Group. Julia Hilton (Green Party) leads the Green Party Group. Paul Redstone (Conservative) leads the Conservative Group.

The SBSA Audit and Governance Committee discussed the appointment of a Chair and Vice-Chair, and received an introduction from Grant Thornton regarding external audit. They also reviewed the internal audit strategy and the approach to the Treasury Management Policy for 2027/28. Johnny Denis (Green Party) declared an interest as the Lewes District Council Cabinet member for community wellbeing, arts, culture, tourism and leisure, stating he did not consider it prejudicial.

In the next fortnight, the People Scrutiny Committee will meet on 23 November to discuss the Safeguarding Children Partnership Annual Report and the Crisis and Resilience Fund. The East Sussex Health and Wellbeing Board meets on 24 November to review the Health and Social Care Integration Programme and the response to the National Plan to End Homelessness. The Pension Committee meets on 26 November to review the Pension Board Minutes and the LGPS Governance. The Audit Committee meets on 27 November to review the Internal Audit Progress Report and the Auditor's Annual Report. The Place Scrutiny Committee meets on 30 November to review the Strategic Asset Plan and the Queensway Gateway Road post-project appraisal. Full Council meets on 1 December.
