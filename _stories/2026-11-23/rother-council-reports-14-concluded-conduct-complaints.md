Rother District Council’s Standards Committee is due to meet on Tuesday 1 December to review a six-monthly update on councillor conduct.

A report by the council’s Monitoring Officer, Philip Baker, states that 14 Code of Conduct complaints were concluded between June and November 2026. The report notes that the triage stage continues to reduce the resource burden of minor complaints.

One upheld case involved Councillor Connor Winter, following comments made in July 2025. An independent investigation found breaches of the Members' Code of Conduct. Councillor Winter agreed to a local resolution, which included a public-facing statement, written apologies, and refresher training on social media use. The committee supported this resolution in July 2026.

The committee will also review a report on Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman complaints. Mark Adams, Head of Digital and Customer Services, reported that four cases were determined in the period, with one regarding planning applications not investigated due to insufficient evidence of fault.
