Rye Heritage Centre is preparing for the festive season with a new sound and light show titled Smugglers' Christmas: An Adventure Around Rye. The 15-minute production features Captain Chris and his gang on a mission to save Christmas, incorporating immersive effects such as scents and interactive lighting. It is suitable for all ages and takes place in the auditorium where the 50-year-old Rye Town Model is housed.

Tickets cost £3 per child, with one adult free per child. The show runs on Saturdays in December and on specific weekdays from December 21 to 23. Visitors can also explore the free Smugglers' Attic walk-through, designed for those aged five and over, and view the regular Story of Rye show.

As a charity, the centre states that all income from shows and sales goes directly back into maintaining the heritage centre for public benefit. The centre will close from 4pm on Wednesday 23 December, reopening for one day on Saturday 2 January before closing again until late February 2027.
