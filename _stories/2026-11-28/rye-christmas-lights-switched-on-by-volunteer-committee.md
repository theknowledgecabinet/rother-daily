The central streets of Rye have been lit for the festive season following the work of a dedicated volunteer team. The Christmas lights committee, made up of local business owners, mayors, and citizens, installed new lights in the town centre.

Chair Fran Huxley of Simply Rye said the funds raised last year covered this year's costs. She credited Martin and Wendy Summers for organising the installation, noting that Martin's company, Treesawrus, held the necessary public liability insurance. This arrangement reduced the installation cost to a third of the price paid in the previous year.

Martin Summers said the installation took 12 hours on Monday night and six hours on Tuesday. He was joined by two colleagues from his company and a young man named Theo, alongside committee members. The lights are now up in time for late night shopping on Saturday and the Christmas Tree Festival at St Mary’s.
