Dr Kieran Mullan, the MP, has launched a survey to gather evidence of poor postal services in the constituency. He said he has received numerous complaints from residents in Bexhill, Pevensey and Robertsbridge about unacceptable delays, damaged post and missing items.

Dr Mullan stated that late or missing post can have serious consequences when it involves medical appointments, bills and other important correspondence. He clarified that these concerns are not a reflection on local postal workers, but rather a failure by Royal Mail to provide the level of service residents are entitled to expect.

A Royal Mail spokesperson acknowledged the importance of a reliable service in the area. The company cited independently measured figures showing improvements in the BN postcode area, where 83.3 per cent of First Class letters arrived the next working day, up from 71.5 per cent the previous year. In the TN postcode area, performance increased from 66.0 per cent to 83.7 per cent over the same period.
